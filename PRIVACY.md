Gem Player — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Gem Player respects your privacy.
- Data collection: Gem Player does not collect personal information or use analytics or tracking.
- Local storage: App preferences, music library settings, and playlists are stored locally on your device.
- Music files: Gem Player accesses music files and folders that you select. Your music files are not uploaded to external servers.
- Third-party services: Gem Player does not share your data with third parties.
- Contact: For privacy-related questions, please contact the developer through GitHub Issues.

This policy may be updated as the application evolves.
